[B]1.0.0[/B]
- First release.
- 19 date-based holidays, each with its own greeting, colours, backdrop, countdown, effect and home-screen rows: New Year's, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Groundhog Day, Valentine's Day, Presidents' Day, St. Patrick's Day, Easter, Mother's Day, Memorial Day, Juneteenth, Father's Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Talk Like a Pirate Day, Columbus Day, Halloween, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving and Christmas.
- Effects include rolling graveyard fog, falling snow, leaves and shamrocks, rising hearts, petals, confetti and fireworks, plus a New Year's Eve countdown and ball drop.
- Seasons settings page: change any holiday's dates, look, effect and rows, or add up to five holidays of your own.
- Up to three add-ons can be added to the top menu.
- CoreELEC settings, power-menu items and system info on CoreELEC.
